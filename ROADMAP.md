# Roadmap

Where MotionCab is heading: what is being worked on and what is planned
next. The list changes as priorities shift and as player feedback comes in.
What has already shipped is in the [README][readme] and in the
[release notes][releases].

Have an idea that isn't listed? Open a [feature request][issues] or come
talk about it on [Discord][discord].

## In development

- Better Cabin Walk animations
- Look Forward key

## Planned

- Enhance Road Intensity effect
- Enhance Suspension effect
- Community Settings
